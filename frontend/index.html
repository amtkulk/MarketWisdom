Market Wisdom: Indian stock-market research

Market Wisdom is an independent research hub for the Indian stock market. It brings together, in one place, the things an active investor checks every day: live Nifty analysis, a global-market overview, an NSE indices heatmap, multi-factor stock screeners, BSE corporate announcements and market news.

What you can do here

  • Nifty Analysis: trend, RSI, put-call ratio, expected range and Gann levels.
  • Global Market & Heatmap: world indices, commodities, currencies and every NSE index at a glance.
  • Stock screeners: Master Screener, Small/Mid and Micro Cap scanners, Hidden Gems, Wyckoff and the Multibagger Early Signal model.
  • Corporate Actions: the latest BSE order wins, analyst and investor meets, and press releases.
  • REITs & InvITs: listed funds with dividend ex-date alerts.
  • Watchlist: your own list of stocks after signing in with Google.
